How to use CountMeIn

A simple path from install to “we’re all settled.”

1

Create your account

Sign up with email or Google. Set your display name, currency, and (optionally) a monthly spending limit.

2

Track personal spending

Tap + to add an expense, pick a category, and watch your Home summary and Analytics update. Use Search when you need an old entry fast.

3

Start or join a group

Create a group for a trip or flat, share the join code/link, or join one a friend sent you. Add people who aren’t on the app yet as placeholders if needed.

4

Add shared expenses

Log who paid, choose equal/custom/income-weighted splits, or scan a receipt and assign items. For dinners, start a live claim so everyone taps their dishes.

5

Settle up

Open Settle Up to see the simplest payments. Add your payment handles in Profile so friends can pay you the way you actually use. Mark settled when done.

Core splitting stays usable on free. Pro removes ads and unlocks power features for frequent users.

FAQ

Does CountMeIn move money between people?

No. It tracks balances and can show payment handles. Actual transfers happen in your bank/UPI/Venmo/PayPal (or similar) apps.

Is my data private?

Group members see shared group content by design. We don’t sell your personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Can I use it outside India?

Yes—CountMeIn is built for international users with multi-currency and multiple regional settle methods.

How do I delete my account?

Use in-app account deletion where available, or contact us via the feedback form.
