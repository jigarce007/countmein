Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026
CountMeIn ("CountMeIn", "we", "us", or "our") is an expense tracking and bill-splitting application designed to help you manage everyday spending, budgets, and shared expenses. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information CountMeIn handles, how it is used, and how third-party services integrated into the app may process information.

1. Information Stored by Spendly

CountMeIn allows you to record and manage financial information such as:

  • Expenses
  • Expense categories
  • Budgets
  • Split bills and shared expense information
  • Dates and amounts associated with expenses
  • Other information you choose to enter into the app

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide CountMeIn's expense tracking, budgeting, reporting, and bill-splitting features.

CountMeIn does not currently provide bank account connectivity, bank transaction synchronization, or cloud backup/synchronization of your expense data.

We do not sell your personally entered expense or bill information.

2. Analytics

CountMeIn uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to understand how users interact with the application and to improve the app.

Analytics may include information such as:

  • App launches and sessions
  • General app usage and interactions
  • Device and app information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Aggregated usage statistics

We use analytics to understand metrics such as daily and weekly active users, app retention, feature usage, and overall application performance.

We do not intentionally send the contents of your personal expenses, expense titles, merchant names, bank information, or other financial records to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase may process information according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

3. Advertising

CountMeIn currently displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google.

AdMob may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App and device information
  • Advertising and interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share certain device, interaction, diagnostic, and identifier information automatically.

4. Push Notifications

CountMeIn may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications to your device.

Push notifications may be used for useful app-related information such as:

  • Budget alerts
  • Spending insights
  • Important application notifications

FCM may process information required to deliver notifications to the appropriate device, such as an application/device registration token.

CountMeIn also uses local, on-device notifications for certain expense and budget-related reminders. These notifications are generated using information stored locally on your device.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Information We Do Not Currently Collect Through CountMeIn

CountMeIn currently does not provide:

  • Bank account connections
  • Bank transaction synchronization
  • Credit or debit card account connections
  • Cloud storage of your expense records
  • Cloud backup of your expense records
  • Cross-device expense synchronization
  • Paid subscriptions or Pro accounts
  • A CountMeIn account/login system for storing your expense records on our servers

If these features are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Third-Party Services

CountMeIn uses third-party services to provide certain functionality, including:

Google Firebase Analytics Used for application analytics and understanding general app usage.
Firebase Cloud Messaging Used to deliver push notifications.
Google AdMob Used to display advertisements and support advertising-related functionality.

These third-party services may collect and process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CountMeIn

Financial information entered into CountMeIn is primarily stored locally on your device. The security of locally stored information also depends on the security mechanisms provided by your device and operating system.

Information transmitted by third-party services such as analytics, advertising, and push-notification services is handled according to the security practices of those providers.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Expense, budget, and split-bill information stored locally by CountMeIn remains on your device until you delete it through the app or remove the application's local data or uninstall the application.

Because CountMeIn currently does not store your expense records on our own servers, we do not maintain a cloud copy of those records.

Information processed by third-party services such as analytics, advertising, and push notifications may be retained by those providers according to their respective policies and retention practices.

9. Children's Privacy

CountMeIn is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through CountMeIn for our own purposes. If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us.

10. Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may have choices regarding:

  • Notification permissions
  • Advertising personalization
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Analytics collection where applicable
  • Deleting locally stored CountMeIn data

You can manage many of these choices through your Android device settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CountMeIn's functionality, data practices, or third-party services change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
