FAQ

Quick answers before you install.

Does CountMeIn transfer money?

No. It tracks balances and can show payment handles. Real transfers happen in your bank/UPI/Venmo/PayPal apps.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. CountMeIn is built for users worldwide, with multi-currency support and regional settle methods (UPI, Venmo, PayNow, PayPal, and more).

How is my data handled?

Group members see shared group content by design. We don’t sell your personal data. Read the Privacy Policy.
