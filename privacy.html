1.Overview

Spendly is a personal and group expense-tracking application. This Policy applies to all users of the App and describes our practices regarding personal data processed through the App, its cloud backend (Google Firebase), and associated third-party services (advertising and in-app billing).

We act as the data controller for personal data processed through the App, except where a third-party service provider (such as Google) acts as an independent controller for data it collects directly, as described in Section 8.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not request or store bank account numbers, card numbers, or other direct payment credentials. Any in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we only receive confirmation of purchase status, not your payment details.

3.How Information Is Collected

  • Directly from you — when you set a display name, enter expenses, create or join a group, send a chat message, or contact support.
  • Automatically — Firebase assigns your device an anonymous authentication identity and installation ID the first time you open the App, with no email or account sign-in required; the App's use of Firebase SDKs (Analytics, crash/performance logging, App Check) also collects usage and diagnostic data as you use the Service.
  • From third parties — Google Play (purchase confirmation for Spendly Pro, advertising identifiers for the ad-supported free tier).

4.How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and sync the core expense-tracking and group features of the Service;
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account;
  • Enable group creation, membership, expense splitting, settlement calculations, and group chat;
  • Send push notifications relevant to your account or groups (e.g., new expense, group invite, chat message);
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and manage Spendly Pro entitlements;
  • Serve advertisements in the free tier of the App via Google AdMob;
  • Monitor, analyze, and improve App performance, stability, and features (via Firebase Analytics and crash reporting);
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your expense content, financial data, or chat messages to train third-party advertising models, and we do not share your raw financial data with advertisers.

5.Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we process personal data on the following grounds: (a) performance of a contract, to provide the Service you requested; (b) your consent, for example for notifications, camera access, or advertising identifiers, which you may withdraw at any time via device settings; (c) legitimate interests, such as securing the Service, preventing fraud, and improving the App; and (d) compliance with legal obligations, where required.

6.How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share information as follows:

  • With other users of a Group — expense data, split/settlement information, group chat messages, and your name/profile photo are visible to other members of any Group you join, as described in Section 7;
  • With service providers — Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, messaging, functions, analytics, app integrity), Google Play (billing), and Google AdMob (advertising), each of which processes data under its own privacy terms as our processor or, for advertising, as an independent controller;
  • For legal reasons — where required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Infinity Fintech Solutions, our users, or the public;
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality arrangements.

7.Groups & Chat Data

Group features are, by design, shared and collaborative. If you create or join a Group:

  • Your name, profile photo, expense entries, and chat messages posted within that Group are visible to all current members of the Group, and may remain visible to members who joined the Group after the content was posted;
  • Group administrators may be able to remove members or delete the Group, which may permanently delete associated data for all members;
  • We store Group data (including chat messages) on Google Firebase/Firestore to enable real-time synchronization across members' devices;
  • We do not actively monitor the content of group chat messages, but we may access stored data as necessary to operate the Service, respond to abuse reports, enforce our Terms, or comply with legal obligations.

You should not share sensitive personal, financial account, or identity information within group chat, as it is visible to other group members.

9.Advertising & AdMob

The free tier of the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. You can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset/limit your advertising identifier, through your device's operating system settings (e.g., Android Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Upgrading to Spendly Pro removes in-app advertisements and the associated ad-serving data collection.

11.Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide the Service. Group data (including chat messages) is retained for as long as the Group exists or until removed by a Group administrator. We may retain certain data for a limited period after account deletion where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or as reflected in routine backup cycles, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

12.Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls, Firebase Security Rules restricting data access to authorized users, and device attestation (App Check) to reduce abuse from unauthorized clients. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and account credentials.

16.Account & Data Deletion

Spendly does not use email/password or third-party sign-in accounts; each installation of the App is identified only by an anonymous, device-bound Firebase identity as described in Section 2. As a result, "account deletion" works differently than in apps with traditional logins:

  • On-device data — you can remove all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its app storage from your device settings. This ends your current anonymous session; a new, unrelated anonymous identity is created if you reinstall or reopen the App.
  • Cloud-synced data — uninstalling the App does not automatically delete data already synced to our cloud backend (such as Groups you created or joined, expense entries, and chat messages), because this data may still be needed by, or visible to, other members of your Groups.
  • Requesting cloud data deletion — to request deletion of the data associated with your device's anonymous identity from our backend, contact us at the email in Section 18 with reasonably identifying details (e.g., Group name(s), display name(s) used, and approximate dates of use) so we can locate and delete it. We will process valid requests within a reasonable period, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Please note that content you shared within a Group (such as chat messages or expense entries already visible to other members) may remain visible to those members even after your underlying data is deleted from our backend, unless separately removed by a Group administrator.
