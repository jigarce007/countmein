1.Overview

CountMeIn is a personal and shared expense-tracking application for users worldwide. It helps you log personal spending, manage budgets and analytics, create or join groups, split expenses (including itemized receipt splits and live item claims), settle balances, use optional features such as Spot Me IOUs, recurring expenses, referrals, home-screen widgets, and optional CountMeIn Pro subscriptions.

We act as the data controller for personal data we process through the Service, except where a third-party provider (for example Google) acts as an independent controller for data it collects under its own policies, as described below.

Important: CountMeIn is a record-keeping and coordination tool. We are not a bank, payment institution, or money transmitter. We do not hold user funds or execute peer-to-peer transfers inside the App.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect / Process as a Payment Provider

We do not request full bank account numbers or payment card numbers to move money between users. In-app purchases (CountMeIn Pro) are processed by Google Play Billing (and, where applicable, Apple In-App Purchase). We receive entitlement/purchase status, not your full payment card details.

3.How Information Is Collected

  • Directly from you — registration, profile setup, expenses, groups, chat, uploads, payment handles, referrals, and support requests.
  • Automatically — when you use the App, through authentication, analytics, crash reporting, App Check, notifications, and (if enabled) location prompts.
  • From third parties — Google Sign-In profile basics you authorize; Google Play / App Store purchase confirmations; ad networks for the free tier.
  • On-device processing — receipt text recognition / OCR may run on your device using on-device libraries; derived amounts or line items may then be saved to your account or group if you confirm them.

4.How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, sync, and improve personal expense tracking, budgets, analytics, recurring expenses, widgets, and related features;
  • Enable groups, invitations, splits (including itemized and live item claim), settlements, Spot Me, chat, and notifications;
  • Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized clients (including App Check);
  • Process optional Pro subscriptions and manage entitlements/referral rewards;
  • Serve and measure advertisements on free tiers;
  • Analyze stability and usage to fix bugs and improve the product;
  • Provide optional location-based expense reminders when you opt in;
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your expense amounts, chat contents, or receipt images to sell personalized ads based on the contents of those records.

5.Legal Basis for Processing

Where required (for example under GDPR or similar laws), we process data based on: (a) performance of a contract to provide the Service; (b) consent (notifications, camera/photos, location, certain advertising identifiers—withdrawable in device or App settings); (c) legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and product improvement; and (d) legal obligations.

6.How We Share Information

  • Other group members — profile details, expenses, splits, settlements, chat, and related content in groups you join are visible to those members;
  • Service providers — Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check), Google Play / App Store billing, Google AdMob, and similar infrastructure providers;
  • Legal / safety — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security;
  • Business transfers — in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

7.Groups, Chat & Shared Data

Shared features are collaborative by design. Content you post in a group may remain visible to current and future members. Group admins/hosts may manage membership and group settings. We do not continuously moderate chat, but may access stored data to operate the Service, investigate abuse, enforce Terms, or comply with law.

Do not post passwords, full payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive data in chat or expense notes.

8.Receipts, OCR & Media

If you scan or upload a receipt or photo: (a) the image may be processed on-device and/or uploaded to secure cloud storage associated with your account or group; (b) OCR results can be inaccurate—you are responsible for reviewing totals and line items before saving; (c) receipt images shared in a group context may be visible to group members.

9.Location Features

Location-triggered expense prompts are optional and, where offered, intended for foreground use while you are using the App. We do not require location to use core expense or group features. You can deny or revoke location permission in system settings; doing so disables location-based prompts.

10.Payment Handles & Settlements

Optional payment handles help other users know how to pay you outside the App. Settlements between users occur outside CountMeIn. We do not verify that an external payment was completed unless you mark it as settled in the App. Handles you save may be visible to relevant group members for settle-up convenience.

14.Data Retention

We retain account and service data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Group content may persist while the group exists or while needed by other members. After deletion requests, we delete or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, dispute, or backup purposes.

15.Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls, Firebase Security Rules, and App Check. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, account credentials, and Google account.

16.Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict/object to certain processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us (Section 21). You can also manage notifications, permissions, ad settings, and in-app account deletion where available. We may verify identity before fulfilling requests.

17.Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under the age of legal majority where you live, use the Service only with a parent/guardian's consent. Contact us to request deletion of data collected from a child under 13 without proper consent.

18.International Data Transfers

CountMeIn is offered internationally. Data may be processed in countries where we or our providers operate (including outside your country of residence). Those countries may have different data-protection laws. We rely on appropriate provider safeguards for such transfers.

19.Account & Data Deletion

  • Use in-app account deletion where available, or email us at the address below.
  • Deleting your account removes or anonymizes personal account data we control, subject to legal/backup exceptions.
  • Content you shared in groups (expenses, chat, photos) may remain visible to other members until removed by group processes or separate requests where feasible.
  • Uninstalling the App alone does not always delete cloud data needed by other group members.
